01 · ABOUT ME

A little about me.

The person behind the models: what drives me, how I work, and the road that brought me here.

Hi, I'm Nabin, a Machine Learning and AI engineer with a Master's in Computer Science from Wichita State University and a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science from Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology, Bangalore.

My path into machine learning took shape as a Machine Learning with Python Intern at YBI Foundation, where I found what I love most: exploring data, cleaning it, and turning it into models that make real predictions.

In my final year of engineering, my team and I built a system that lets you control a computer with hand gestures, and that work was published by Springer. It was my first taste of research. As a Graduate Research Assistant at Wichita State University, I built an OCR and machine learning system that reads scanned admissions documents, turns them into applicant profiles and supports Admit / Review / Deny decisions, presenting my results directly to faculty advisors.

What I bring to a team: curiosity, patience with hard problems, and care for the full picture: clean data, the right model, honest evaluation, and how it all fits into a real system. I enjoy collaborating, and I'm always happy to connect with engineers, researchers and recruiters working in AI.
