A little about me.

The person behind the models: what drives me, how I work, and the road that brought me here.

Hi, I'm Nabin, a Machine Learning and AI engineer based in Wichita, Kansas. I'm completing my Master's in Computer Science at Wichita State University, after earning my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science from Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology, Bangalore.

What drives me is the moment an idea stops being a notebook and starts helping someone: saving hours of manual work, flagging a hidden threat, or letting you control a computer with a wave of your hand. I'm curious by nature, and I genuinely enjoy learning new technologies and staying with a hard problem until it makes sense.

How I work: I care about the full picture, not just the accuracy number. Clean data, the right model for the job, and honest evaluation come first. A background in computer networking and web development means I think about how a model fits into a real system and how people will use it.

Beyond the code, I love collaborating with teams and I'm always happy to connect with engineers, researchers and recruiters working in AI and tech.
